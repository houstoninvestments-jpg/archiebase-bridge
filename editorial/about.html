A little about the person behind the site

My first business had two wheels.And a lawn mower.

I was eleven. I towed the mower behind my bike around the neighborhood, trying to build something of my own. I didn't know where it would lead. I just loved making things work, learning as I went and seeing what I could figure out.

The long way here

Turns out every kind of work teaches you something.

Since those lawn-mowing days I've worked in restaurants, on job sites, around people and behind computers. I've lived and worked in different places, taken on very different jobs, and chased more ideas than I could fit on a résumé.

I've learned what it feels like to be the person actually doing the work. A ringing phone when you're busy with a customer. A clunky process everyone puts up with. A tool that looks great in a demo but makes your real day harder.

That's the part I care about. I like designing, building, connecting tools and figuring out where things can work better. I don't need every problem to turn into an AI project, and I don't believe the most complicated answer is automatically the best one.

I'm a generalist by nature. That means I can look at the customer experience, the words on the website, the system behind it and the person who has to use it. Some areas are strengths, some are things I'm still learning, and some need someone more specialized. I'll be straightforward about the difference.
