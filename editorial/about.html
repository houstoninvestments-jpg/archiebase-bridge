01 / THE BACKSTORY

I've made drinks, climbed roofs, driven routes, and built digital things.

I've worked with all sorts of people in all sorts of environments. Some jobs were incredible. Some were dirty, exhausting, or tough on the body. I didn't always stay long when the work wasn't right for me, but I took something useful away from almost all of it.

"I wasn't necessarily wandering.
I was collecting tools."

I've never been able to leave a problem alone. Why does this take five steps? Who decided this was normal? What if we tried it differently? That kind of curiosity followed me into every job, every hobby, and eventually into building with AI.

03 / THE CONNECTION

From actual workdays to better systems.

Years in hospitality taught me customer service. Hard physical work taught me to respect people's time. Building things taught me how to turn an idea into something real. AI finally gave me a way to connect all of those interests.

01

I understand the messy middle.

Not just what a business looks like on a laptop. The phone that rings while both hands are busy. The customer who needs an answer. The process that everybody works around.

02

I like the problem more than the buzzword.

Whether the answer is a better page, a simple automation, a clearer message, or an AI tool, the technology should serve the business. Not the other way around.

03

I care how the finished thing feels.

Useful is the minimum. I want the work to be thoughtful, original, and better than somebody expected when they asked for help.
