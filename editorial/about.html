01 / THE BACKSTORY
Curiosity
has always been
the engine.

I wasn't collecting jobs.
I was collecting perspectives.

I've worked behind bar counters and across busy dining rooms. On job sites where the work follows you home in your shoulders. On the road, between deliveries and destinations. At events where everything has to come together at exactly the right moment. I've helped build an experience from scratch in Ecuador, and I've built digital things from wherever I could open a laptop, including an RV.

Some of those days were incredible. Others were dirty, exhausting, occasionally demoralizing, and very effective at making me question my life choices. I rarely stayed somewhere just to stay somewhere. If it wasn't a fit, I'd follow my curiosity to the next thing.

But I was paying attention. To the people. To the little details that made a customer's day. To the systems that made a good team great, and the ones that made everyone's work harder than it needed to be.

I didn't learn business from a distance. I spent years on the inside of the work.

03 / A VERY SERIOUS BRAND ORIGIN STORY

Who's Archie,
anyway?

I wanted to build something genuinely useful for small-business owners and give away as much of that value as I could for free. Practical answers. Real starting points. No need to hire me just to get something helpful.

Then came the question every new project eventually asks: What do we call this thing?

I remembered I had an old domain sitting around from one of my many ideas that never quite went anywhere. Archiebase.com.

So the domain got a second life, Archie got a job, and here we are.
