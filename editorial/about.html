My first businesshad two wheels.And a lawn mower.

I started building businesses at eleven. My first was a lawn-mowing operation, with the mower towed behind my bicycle around the neighborhood. I've been hooked ever since.

Since then, I've worked in more places and learned more things than I can fit on a résumé. Washington, Idaho, Oregon, Texas, Hawaii, Ecuador, even from an RV. Different work, same curiosity: what if there's a better way?

01 / THE ROAD HERE

Different places.
Different work.
Same curiosity.

That mower behind my bike was the first of many projects. Since then, I've worked behind bars and across dining rooms. On job sites where you feel every hour in your body. On the road, at events, and in places where everything changes by the day. I've helped create something from scratch in Ecuador, and I've built digital projects from wherever I could open a laptop, including an RV.

Some jobs were incredible. Others were dirty, exhausting, and occasionally pretty effective at making me question my life choices. I rarely stayed somewhere just to stay somewhere. When something wasn't right, curiosity usually pulled me toward the next thing.

But I was paying attention. To the people. To how businesses actually run when nobody is watching the dashboard. The handoffs that fall apart, the workarounds everyone learns to live with, and the tiny details that make customers feel genuinely taken care of.

It turns out the scenic route was teaching me things I couldn't have learned sitting behind a desk.

A very serious branding origin story

Who's
Archie?

I wanted to build something genuinely useful for small-business owners. Something where people could get help, find practical ideas, and walk away with something valuable even if they never hired me.

Then came the inevitable question: What are we calling this thing?

I'd love to tell you the name came from months of strategic brand workshops and deep market psychology.

But I'd had an old domain sitting around from another idea that never quite went anywhere. Archiebase.com.

So the domain got a second life. Archie got a job. And here we are.

A highly sophisticated naming strategy, obviously.
