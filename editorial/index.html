Good questions

The no-nonsense details.

Do I need to know anything about AI?

No. Just tell me what you're trying to improve or build. I choose appropriate tools, explain the approach in ordinary language, and make sure the result is useful.

Is the business review really free?

Yes. It's a concise manual review based on what you share and any relevant public information I can verify. It isn't a complete security, accounting, or internal systems audit. You keep the recommendations even if we never work together.

How do revisions work?

I keep refining the agreed deliverable until you're happy with it. A different product, new pages, extra systems, or major changes to the agreed scope would be priced separately before work starts.

Do I need a call or pay monthly?

Not for a standard project. We can work asynchronously through clear written messages. Any software subscriptions or usage costs belong to your business and are explained before you approve the build.

Did you actually build this website?

Yes. The layout, details, animation, and interactive pieces are an example of the kind of design and development I can bring to your project. We can make yours look and feel like your business, not mine.

What's the difference between an AI agent and an automated workflow?

An automation follows a known sequence, like saving an enquiry and notifying your team. An AI agent can interpret information or answer questions within clear limits. I'll recommend the simpler option when it's enough, and include human review where appropriate.

Can you audit my existing website or business without rebuilding it?

Yes. Start with a free written review for a few practical opportunities. For a deeper plan, the AI Optimization Audit starts at $250 for one website or one defined business workflow. I review what is publicly available and what you choose to share, then explain recommended fixes, tools and likely complexity. I won't pretend to see systems I haven't been given access to.

What if my idea isn't listed?

Send it anyway. I can work across design, websites, AI, content, and automations, and I can learn new tools or suggest a specialist when a project needs it. I'll be upfront if something is outside my capability.
