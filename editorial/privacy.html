Privacy

Here is what happens when you send me a note. It's short on purpose.

What the forms collect

The contact forms ask for a few things:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your business name
  • Your message
  • On some forms, the kind of help you want

That's it. I only use it to read your note and reply to you.

The free first fix tool works only in your browser. It doesn't send your answers anywhere.

How your note reaches me

A third-party email service called FormSubmit delivers your note to my inbox. That service handles your note so it can deliver it. It has its own privacy policy.

Please don't send passwords or private customer records. Email is not the right place for those.

What I never do

  • I never sell your information.
  • I never rent or trade it to anyone.
  • I never add you to a mailing list without asking.

Visit counts

This site uses Vercel Web Analytics to count page visits. It shows me things like which pages get read. It does not tell me who you are.

Questions or deletion

Want me to delete your note? Or have a question? Send a message through the contact form. I'll take care of it.
