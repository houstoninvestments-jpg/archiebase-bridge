Privacy

Here is what happens when you send me a note. It's short on purpose.

What the forms collect

The contact forms ask for a few things:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your business name
  • Your message
  • On some forms, the kind of help you want

That's it. I only use it to read your note and reply to you.

The free first fix tool works only in your browser. It doesn't send your answers anywhere.

When you try the AI first look

The text you enter goes through Vercel’s AI Gateway to an AI provider to generate your question and suggestion. Leave out names, customer records and confidential details. You can add the result to your contact note and edit it before you send it to Aaron.

How your note reaches me

The contact forms send your note by email through FormSubmit. That service delivers the message to me. It handles your note so it can deliver it, and it has its own privacy policy.

Please don't send passwords or private customer records. Email is not the right place for those.

What I never do

  • I never sell your information.
  • I never rent or trade it to anyone.
  • I never add you to a mailing list without asking.

Visit counts

This site does not run a visit counter or an advertising tracker.

Questions or deletion

Want me to delete a note I already have, or have a question? Send a message through the contact form. I'll take care of it.
