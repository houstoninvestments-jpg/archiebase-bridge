A FEW THINGS I'VE HEARD BEFORE

Sometimes the obvious problem isn't the real one.

Every business has its own quirks. My job isn't to hand you a service menu and make you pick. It's to understand the friction, then find what would actually help.

01

"The website looks fine. So why isn't it bringing in customers?"

Maybe it's not the design.

Maybe people can't tell what you do, why it matters, or how to get started. A better story can sometimes do more than a prettier homepage.

02

"We bought the software. Why is everything still such a pain?"

Maybe the handoff is the problem.

When people keep retyping, chasing and double-checking, I want to trace where work gets stuck before buying another tool.

03

"There's an idea I've had for years. I just don't know how to build it."

Maybe we start smaller and smarter.

A simple working version could let you test the idea before committing to something huge, expensive or overly complicated.

04

"Business is good. I just know it could work better."

That's worth looking at too.

Better customer experiences, sharper branding, smoother systems and fewer unnecessary costs matter even when nothing is on fire.

You don't need to know whether the fix is a website, an automation, AI, or something else.

Just tell me what you're seeing
MY WORKING METHOD

Zoom out. Look closer. Then build.

I love solving problems, but I love understanding them even more. The first idea isn't always the right one, and the most impressive tool isn't always the most useful.

01 /

Listen to how it actually works.

Not the polished version on a flowchart. What really happens on a Tuesday when things get busy? What do customers see? What does your staff work around?

02 /

Find the part that moves the needle.

A confusing sentence, a missing reply, five duplicate steps, an outdated page. I look for the underlying issue before deciding what deserves to be built.

03 /

Make the fix fit your business.

Maybe it's custom design, an AI helper, a workflow, a tiny tool or one change you can make yourself. The goal is something better, not something bigger.

"Your business doesn't need another shiny thing. It needs somebody who cares enough to figure out what actually works."

THE PERSON, NOT THE AGENCY

I've spent a lot of timeon both sides of the counter.

I grew up around small businesses. My family had a gutter installation business, and I worked alongside my dad. Other relatives built businesses in finance, technology, construction and small restaurants. I saw how much gets handled behind the scenes before I ever built anything with AI.

Since then I've crawled under sinks, worked on roofs, driven screws into decks, built things with my hands, made countless drinks behind the bar and spent thousands of hours on the road. I've also built websites, tried online ventures and spent years exploring creative and AI tools.

All of that taught me to notice where people get stuck. The awkward step everyone accepts. The message that doesn't quite land. The thing that looks good on a screen but is a pain in real life.

I'm not pretending to be an expert in everything. I'm a curious, wide-ranging builder who won't stop at the first answer. When something needs a specialist, I'll say so.

Archiebase is the name. I'm the person you actually work with.
