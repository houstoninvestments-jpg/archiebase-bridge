THE SIMPLE ANSWERS

Good to know.

Is the written audit really free?

Yes. You'll receive a short, practical response based on what you tell me and any public business information I can verify. It isn't an automated grade or a full internal operational review. You don't need to buy anything.

Do I have to schedule a call?

No. Standard projects are text-first. Requirements, recommendations, quotes, and updates can happen through written messages and forms. If a project needs specialist access or a deeper discussion, we'll agree on the simplest secure way to handle it.

Are software subscriptions included?

No. Any hosting, phone number, texting, CRM, or automation platform is owned and paid for by your business whenever possible. I'll explain these costs before you approve a project.

What if my problem doesn't fit a package?

I'll tell you. You can still ask for a written audit. If a custom build makes sense, I'll quote its scope separately. I won't turn a simple project into a complicated rebuild.
